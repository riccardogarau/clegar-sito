Operational presence

Owner's Engineering

We represent you where the project is executed. On board during acquisition, on the quay during mobilisation, at the technical table when a decision can't wait until tomorrow.

What it covers

Operational presence and the client's technical voice, with the decision authority delegated to us and the traceability the contract requires.

  • 01Client representativeProtecting the client's interest in day-to-day dealings with the contractor: scope, quality, schedule, cost and non-conformities.
  • 02Offshore representativeOn-board presence for the whole campaign or for the critical phases, with daily reporting and a decision log.
  • 03MobilisationQuayside verification of equipment, certificates, calibrations and system trials. We sign off mobilisation only when the conditions are genuinely there.
  • 04WitnessingDocumented attendance at calibrations, acceptance tests and critical verifications, with evidence that stands up in dispute.
  • 05Operational supervisionProduction and downtime control, online QC, management of deviations and variation requests as they happen.
